# AutoZ - Vehicle Marketplace & Auctions
USA Sponsored Project by #Tech Tutor

### 3 Main Modules (Company Level)
1.  **Marketplace:** Car listing with price (Swift 3.5L, Scorpio 12.5L)
2.  **Verification:** Admin checks Engine, Papers -> Verified Badge
3.  **Live Auction:** Bidding system + Countdown Timer (Bids: 1 - 4.1L wala logic)
